# Profile: drone / aircraft, 10 Hz
# Airborne <4g dynamic model at 10 Hz. Portable and automotive models assume small vertical
# speeds and can lag or reject fixes during climbs and fast maneuvers; this one doesn't.
# ArduPilot and PX4 configure the receiver themselves unless auto-config is turned off.
# At 10 Hz the factory NMEA output (~870 bytes per fix) needs 115200 baud or more; at 38400
# the link saturates and replies get dropped. Or combine with output-nmea-minimal.

CFG-NAVSPG-DYNMODEL 8
CFG-RATE-MEAS 100
CFG-RATE-NAV 1
